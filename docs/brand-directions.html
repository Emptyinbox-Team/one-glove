One Glove: five brand directions

Same app, same name, warmer heart. Each direction below is a full identity: mark, palette, type pairing and the line we'd put on the poster. Pick the one you'd be proud to wear on a beanie, then leave a note. You can change your vote any time; the tally updates live for everyone.

Voting happens on the live ballot linked in the README; this page is the read-only gallery.
Direction 01

Sunrise Session

A 1970s festival poster: sunburst, open palm, primary warmth. The whole brand says "come as you are, we'll find your other half." Loudest of the five, and the most obviously joyful.

One glove. One love. Come back together.

Type
Righteous for display, Nunito for everything else
Voice
Warm, plain, a little sung. "Your glove's been waiting."
Icon
Red sun, cream glove, gold rays. Reads at 29 px.
Risk
Red-gold-green carries cultural weight; wear it with respect, never with clichés.
Direction 02

Two Hands

Two mittens, two colours, one heart-shaped gap between them. The mark is the reunion itself. Soft, friendly, the kind of brand a parent trusts with a child's lost mitten.

Every glove has a somebody.

Type
Caveat (hand-written) for headlines, Quicksand for UI
Voice
Gentle, first-person, hopeful. Notifications feel like a neighbour knocking.
Icon
The heart-gap between the two mittens, coral on teal.
Risk
Least edgy. Could read as a kids' brand if the copy goes soft.
Direction 03

Knit Together

Wool, embroidery, a badge you could sew on a toque. Cozy Nordic-meets-Canadian, built for ski towns and cold cities. The brand feels like the thing it's looking for.

Lost, found, knit back together.

Type
Fredoka (rounded, chunky) with Nunito body
Voice
Cosy and practical. Reunion cards look like a patch you earned.
Icon
Gold badge with stitched glove; works as a physical pin and a sticker.
Risk
Very winter. Southern-hemisphere summer users may feel it's not for them.
Direction 04

Good Vibrations

A glove waving hello, sending out rings of colour like a radio signal. Black ground makes the three colours sing. Street-poster energy, built for stickers, tees and a 9:16 story.

Wave. Find. Reunite.

Type
Bebas Neue display, Work Sans for body and UI
Voice
Short, rhythmic, upbeat. Three-word sentences. Big on sound and motion in-app.
Icon
Cream glove on black with the three rings; the most distinctive on a home screen.
Risk
Dark UI by default; the three-stripe palette must stay abstract, never costume.
Direction 05

Warm Bureau

The case-file idea from the brief, with the heat turned up: kraft paper, sunflower tape, a red REUNITED stamp. Keeps the deadpan humour for the posters, saves the warmth for the moment it matters.

Case closed. Hearts open.

Type
Special Elite (typewriter) for headlines and stamps, Nunito for UI
Voice
Deadpan bureau on the poster, sincere on the reunion card. Both jokes land.
Icon
Kraft folder with the stamp; feels like a real object.
Risk
Least "One Love." Warm through props, not colour. The middle path may satisfy no one.
