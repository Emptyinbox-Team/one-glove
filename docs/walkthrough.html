01 · The pitch

Nobody has built the matching layer.

Gloves are the one lost item people already photograph and joke about. Instagram accounts, NPR segments and Tom Hanks have all made the joke. No one has closed the loop: found glove, meet lost glove.

Why gloves

Universally relatable, slightly absurd, photographs well. The joke has legs.

Why now

Share sheets, on-device image embeddings and App Clips make it a 20-second loop.

Why here

Whistler and the Sea-to-Sky corridor: dense, glove-heavy, six-month season from November.

02 · Brand and tone

04 · Core loop

Post. Match. Reunite. Share.

Under a minute of user effort. The found side is the engine: finders have zero stake, so a Witness Report is one photo and one tap, no account.

Reunion cardShare sheet, finder gets credit

Key insight: a lost post includes a photo of the survivor, the glove you still have. We match glove to glove, not glove to memory.

06 · Matching

The app proposes. Humans confirm.

Hand

Hard filter. A left survivor only matches a right found. Halves the search.

Geo + time

Within radius (default 5 km), found on or after last-seen, 60-day window.

Attributes

Glove or mitten, colour, material, adult or child. On-device classifier.

Visual similarity

On-device image embedding, pgvector cosine distance, also compared against the mirrored survivor.

Confirm

"That's it" from both sides opens Handoff. "Not mine" is never resurfaced.

Learn

Confirmed reunions become labelled pairs; weights get tuned after season one.

score = 0.45·sim + 0.20·geo + 0.15·time + 0.20·attr  ·  hand mismatch ⇒ 0

07 · Screens

Seven screens. Nothing else.

The Wall

Home. Nearby cases and reports, map or list, big Report button.

Report

Found glove. Camera, pin, one optional line. No login.

Open a Case

Lost glove. Survivor photo, last-seen pin and date, radius.

Case File

Evidence photo, case number, map, candidates, share poster.

Matches

Side-by-side pairs. "That's it" or "Not mine".

Chat + Handoff

Minimal chat, "where I left it" pin, mark reunited.

Reunion

Card, share sheet, rank progress.

Sign in with Apple

Only when chatting or confirming. Everything else is anonymous.

08 · Tech

SwiftUI front. Supabase back. No servers.

LayerChoice
AppSwiftUI, Swift 6, iOS 17+, MVVM with Observation
VisionVision hand pose + subject lift, Core ML feature print, all on device
BackendSupabase: Postgres + PostGIS + pgvector, Storage, Auth, Edge Functions, Realtime
PushAPNs via Edge Function on a database webhook
Share assetsSwiftUI ImageRenderer to PNG and PDF
App ClipReport flow only, launched from poster QR codes

Tables: profiles, posts, matches, messages, reunions. Matching is a Postgres function called on every new post. Repo: one-glove/ with ios/ and supabase/ side by side.
